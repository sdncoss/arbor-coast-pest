Privacy Policy

Arbor Coast Pest Solutions ("Arbor Coast," "we," "us") provides pest control services in Orange County, California. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.


Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, and property address.
  • Details about your pest concern, including any notes you add to our quote request form.
  • Records of the services we perform and our communications with you, including text messages.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends automatically when you visit our website, such as browser type and device type.

How we use your information

  • To respond to quote requests and schedule inspections and treatments.
  • To perform and follow up on services, including appointment confirmations, reminders, and service updates.
  • To provide customer support and answer your questions.
  • To keep our records, meet legal obligations, and improve our website and services.

Text messaging information

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive text messages, we use that number only to send the messages described in our Terms & Conditions below. Mobile information and text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories of information described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.


How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business and serve you:

  • Service providers. Companies that help us operate, such as the service that delivers our website form submissions to us, and our messaging and communications providers. They may use your information only to perform services for us.
  • Licensed partners. If your job requires a licensed partner, such as a fumigation company for whole-structure termite tenting, we share the details they need to complete the work.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law or to protect our rights, customers, or the public.

Retention and security

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide services and meet business and legal requirements, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.


Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting us below, and you can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. California residents may have additional rights under California privacy law; contact us to make a request.


Changes to this policy
